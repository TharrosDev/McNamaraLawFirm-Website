The full breadth of Ontario estate law.

From a first will to a multi-generational plan — every service is delivered with the same patient, considered approach.

01
Wills & Testaments

A will that does what you mean it to do.

A well-drafted will is the single most useful document most adults will ever sign. We prepare last wills and testaments for individuals, couples and families — from straightforward plans to wills involving foreign assets, beneficiaries outside Canada, business interests, farm property, and the support of dependent loved ones.

  • Single & mirror wills for couples
  • Wills with testamentary trusts
  • Multi-jurisdictional planning
  • Business succession provisions
  • Guardianship designations for minors
  • Priority & emergency drafting
02
Powers of Attorney

Authority where you choose it, when you cannot act yourself.

Ontario's Continuing Powers of Attorney for Property and for Personal Care let you appoint someone you trust to make financial and health decisions on your behalf if illness, accident or age prevents you from doing so. We prepare both, explain their scope, and counsel on how to choose attorneys wisely.

  • Continuing Power of Attorney for Property
  • Power of Attorney for Personal Care
  • Custom limitations & conditions
  • Springing & immediate authority
  • Multiple-attorney structures
  • Counsel for attorneys acting under POA
03
Probate & Estate Administration

Steady help through a difficult time.

When a loved one passes, the Ontario Superior Court of Justice often requires a Certificate of Appointment of Estate Trustee — commonly called probate — before the estate can be administered. We prepare the application, manage the court process and support estate trustees through their duties from start to finish.

  • Certificates of Appointment (with & without a will)
  • Estate Information Returns
  • Notices to beneficiaries
  • Trustee guidance & advice
  • Estate accounting support
  • Complex or contested matters
04
Estate Planning

Plans that fit your family — not the other way around.

Estate planning is more than a will. We build integrated plans for blended families, business owners, farm families, parents of children with disabilities, and clients with cross-border assets — coordinating with accountants and financial advisors where it helps.

  • Blended-family planning
  • Business succession
  • Farm property planning
  • Henson trusts for dependent beneficiaries
  • Foreign assets & beneficiaries
  • Dependent support obligations
05
Testamentary Trusts

Long-term protection, drafted with care.

A trust created within a will can protect minor beneficiaries, preserve disability benefits, manage tax exposure or stage inheritances over time. We draft trusts that fit a real family — and counsel the trustees who will eventually administer them.

  • Trusts for minor children
  • Henson & absolute-discretion trusts
  • Spousal & spouse-bypass trusts
  • Spendthrift & staged-distribution trusts
  • Charitable testamentary gifts
  • Trustee selection & advice
06
Notary & Commissioner Services

Notarial services, by appointment.

As a Barrister & Solicitor, Erin McNamara is also a Notary Public and Commissioner of Oaths. We provide notarial services for individuals who need official certification of documents, statements or signatures.

  • Notarial (certified true) copies
  • Affidavits & statutory declarations
  • Travel-consent letters for minors
  • Passport & document guarantor
  • Witnessing of agreements
  • Commissioning of oaths
07

Education for groups, workplaces and communities.

Erin regularly delivers estate planning seminars to community groups, workplaces, professional firms and retirement communities across Eastern Ontario — covering wills, powers of attorney, probate and the questions most families never get around to asking.

  • Lunch & learn sessions
  • Community group presentations
  • Retirement-community seminars
  • Continuing-education for professionals
  • Q&A sessions tailored to your audience
  • In-person or virtual delivery
