Our Practice

Estate law, done with intention.

Every estate plan we draft begins with listening — to your family, your values, and the legacy you wish to leave behind. From there, we build a plan that holds up when it matters most.

Wills & Testaments

From straightforward last wills to multi-trust testamentary plans for complex family situations, foreign assets and business succession.

Powers of Attorney

Carefully drafted Continuing Powers of Attorney for Property and Personal Care, so the people you trust can act if you cannot.

Probate Applications

Certificates of Appointment of Estate Trustee — prepared and shepherded through the Ontario Superior Court of Justice on your behalf.

Learn more

Estate Planning

Integrated plans for blended families, dependent loved ones, business owners, farm property and beneficiaries outside Canada.

Testamentary Trusts

Trusts that take effect under a will — for minor children, beneficiaries with disabilities, spendthrift heirs and tax-effective transfers.

Meet Your Counsel

Erin McNamara, B.A., LL.B.

How We Work

A clear, considered process.

From the first phone call to the signed documents safely on file, the path is deliberate, well-paced and respectful of the gravity of the work.

01

Initial Conversation

A no-obligation call to understand your family, your assets and what you need to put in place.

02

Strategy & Plan

We outline the documents required and the structure of the plan — in plain language, with fees disclosed up front.

03

Drafting & Review

Documents are drafted with care, then reviewed with you line by line so nothing is unfamiliar at signing.

04

Signing & Safekeeping

Execution in office, virtually or by house call — followed by safe storage of originals and a tidy client package.
