Principal

Erin McNamara, B.A., LL.B.

"Estate law is, at its heart, a conversation about love and responsibility. The documents are just the form that conversation takes."

Erin McNamara is a Barrister & Solicitor and the principal of McNamara Law Services. A graduate of Queen's Faculty of Law, she has dedicated her career to the practice of estate law — wills, powers of attorney, probate, trusts and the full breadth of planning that follows from them.

In addition to private practice, Erin serves as an Adjunct Professor at the University of Ottawa Faculty of Law, where she teaches Wills and the Law of Succession. Teaching the next generation of Ontario lawyers keeps her practice sharp; her practice keeps her teaching practical.

Outside the office, Erin has long supported the community through pro bono legal work with Reach Canada (now Able2), a charity providing legal services to people with disabilities. She formerly served on the organization's Board of Directors.
